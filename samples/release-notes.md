# Harbor Lights 1.4 — Release notes

Thanks for sailing with us. This update brings the night market,
a calmer fishing loop, and a lot of small fixes.

## New
- The night market opens after 8 pm on the east pier.
- Lanterns now light the path home when it rains.
- You can rename your boat at the harbor office.

## Changed
- Fishing takes fewer clicks. Hold to cast, release to reel in.
- The map remembers the last place you zoomed to.

## Fixed
- Crates no longer fall through the dock at low tide.
- Saving during a storm no longer skips the next morning.
